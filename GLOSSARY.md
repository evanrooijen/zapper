# TV favorites

A user's favorite TV shows and the lists they share with others.

## Language

**Favorites list**:
A user-owned collection of favorite TV shows whose visibility applies to the whole collection.
_Avoid_: Watchlist

**Public list**:
A favorites list that anyone, including visitors without an account, can view through its URL. All entries are public together.

**Private list**:
A favorites list visible only to its owner.

**Favorite entry**:
A TV show included in a favorites list, identified by its IMDb ID.
